    About

    This page is the front end of a small watcher that looks for data analyst internships and posts new ones to a Discord channel. Every half hour it reads the Simplify and Jobright internship lists and polls the job boards of 60 companies directly, then keeps whatever mentions analytics, data, or business intelligence in the title and is based in the United States.

    A listing appears here as soon as the watcher notices it and drops off two weeks after it was first posted. When the same role shows up in more than one place, the copy from the company’s own board is kept so the link goes to the original posting. The watcher collects only what the boards expose in their listing metadata, so the full description lives on the company’s site.
